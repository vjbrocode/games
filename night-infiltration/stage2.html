Mission 2 — Kestrel Point Coastal Battery

0340 hours, forty minutes after Ravenscar. The mainframe you stole names a hardened anti-ship battery on the coast beyond the mountains. Three launchers, one fire-control bunker, and a garrison that has not heard the news yet.
Blow the launchers. Take the targeting keys. Be on the lighthouse pad when Paladin comes back for you.

Sprinting drains stamina; crouching makes you harder to spot. A round through the head or neck drops any man, heavies included.

WEAPONS

1 suppressed pistol   2 MP7 suppressed SMG   3 M4 carbine (N fires the underbarrel M203)   4 .50 anti-materiel rifle   5 AA-12 auto shotgun   6 M249 machine gun   7 combat knife   8 RPG-7   9 suppressed .338 sniper (long range, silent, shoots through glass).
You land with the pistol, the MP7, the .338, the knife and three C4 charges. Everything else is on the map — or on the men carrying it. The .50 punches through sandbags, walls and a sentry turret's armour, and one round sinks a patrol boat's engine.

DEMOLITION

Walk up to a launcher and hold E to plant a charge. Three charges planted — press T to detonate. A detonation wakes the whole point. Plant all three quietly first, then decide when the night ends.

THE RULES OF THE POINT

The comms station on the north hill is how they call for help. Level it (C4 or an RPG round) and no relief boat or truck ever comes.
The fire-control bunker is the mission. Hack the console before anything you do buries it.
Sentry turrets guard both battery gates. Shoot the sensor head, or go around — the cliff ladders below the plateau and the storm drain in the east wire are unwatched.
