Mission 1 — Forward Operating Base Ravenscar

0200 hours. Six cameras are the alarm system. Blind all six and the garrison can never call for help again — leave one live and five tanks cross the ridge. Five sit on the base. The sixth is on a mast out past the north ridge: that one is a scope shot from high ground. Use the silenced pistol up close; the sniper wakes people up.

TACTICAL TABLET — M

A live overhead feed of the real ground, not a drawing of it. Drag to pan — the ground stays under the cursor — or nudge with the ARROW keys, which is the most precise way to place the view. Wheel to zoom. Hold SHIFT while dragging, arrowing or zooming for a quarter-speed precision gear.
R recentre, L labels, I imagery on/off, T overlay on/off, K motion tracks on/off.
It is real time. The world does not stop while you read it — patrols keep walking, the column keeps closing, and every contact draws a fading trail behind it and a dashed leader showing where it will be in three seconds. The track table lists the nearest contacts by bearing, range and closing speed. You are standing still while you look, so anyone who can see you can still see you.
Zoom past about 7 px/m and the symbols thin out to rings so you can see the men themselves — count a patrol, read which way each one is facing, tell the rifles from the UZIs. Hover any hostile for his weapon, his state, his heading and speed, and his range.

MISSION ORDER MATTERS

Hack the signals bunker before you destroy it. Level it with the mainframe still inside and the intelligence goes with it — mission failed. A console in the guard house shuts every camera down at once — but the sixth camera is out on a mast beyond the north ridge, and the cleanest way to take it is a scope shot from Tunnel Heights. Blind all six and the alarm is dead for the rest of the night: no relief column is ever dispatched, and any wave still crossing the ridge is the last one you will see. Finish by destroying every target on the list, then walk into the green beacon.

GROUND

Work the treelines and get onto a ridge — West Ridge, East Knoll, North Spur, Signals Overlook, Tunnel Heights and Far Ridge each have a shot onto something worth hitting. You can walk up any of them.
